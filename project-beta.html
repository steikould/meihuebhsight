Project Beta: Coastal Resilience Initiative

My Role: Senior Program Manager

Client & Location: Seaside County, FL

Project Duration: Mar 2020 - Aug 2022

Project Scale: $45M (Planning & Initial Implementation)

Executive Summary

The Core Challenge or Opportunity: Increasing frequency of coastal flooding and erosion threatening critical infrastructure and residential areas. Opportunity to develop a sustainable, multi-faceted resilience strategy.

My Strategic Action: Led a multi-disciplinary team to conduct vulnerability assessments, develop adaptive strategies including living shorelines and dune restoration, and secure community buy-in.

The Headline Result: Secured $30M in federal grant funding based on the developed plan; reduced projected flood damage costs by 25% over 20 years.

Strategic Impact & Business Acumen

Financial Oversight: Managed a $5M planning budget and developed cost estimates for the $40M initial implementation phase. Coordinated grant applications and financial reporting.

Profitability & Margin Enhancement: Optimized resource allocation across various studies and stakeholder workshops, delivering the planning phase 10% under budget.

Business Development & Client Relations: Built strong trust with county commissioners and community leaders, positioning the firm for future phases of the initiative.

Alignment with Firm's Goals: Expanded the firm's portfolio in climate adaptation and resilience, a growing market sector.

Leadership & Team Management

Team Structure & Your Leadership: Managed a core team of 10 planners and engineers, and coordinated with 5 external environmental consulting firms.

Mentorship & Talent Development: Guided a lead environmental planner in developing novel community engagement techniques that were highly successful.

High-Stakes Communication & Stakeholder Alignment: Facilitated numerous public meetings, addressing concerns from property owners and environmental groups to achieve consensus on the preferred resilience strategies.

Conflict & Crisis Resolution: Resolved disagreements between engineering design preferences and environmental impact concerns by mediating and proposing hybrid solutions.

Technical Complexity & Innovation

The Defining Technical Challenge: Modeling complex coastal processes and predicting the long-term efficacy of nature-based solutions under various climate change scenarios.

Your Innovative Solution: Pioneered the use of a dynamic adaptive pathway planning (DAPP) approach, allowing for flexible long-term strategies that can be adjusted as conditions change.

Key Metrics & Quantifiable Outcomes

  • Budget: Planning Phase: $5M Budget vs. $4.5M Actual Cost.
  • Schedule: Planning phase completed 2 months ahead of schedule.
  • Safety: N/A for planning phase (field visits conducted safely).
  • Quality/Client Satisfaction: Plan unanimously adopted by County Commission; positive media coverage.
  • Team Performance: High team cohesion and cross-disciplinary collaboration achieved.
  • Financial Result: Secured initial implementation funding; positioned firm favorably for $40M in follow-on design and construction management.
