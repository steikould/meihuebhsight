Project Delta: Water Treatment Plant Upgrade

My Role: Project Executive

Client & Location: Riverbend Utility District, TX

Project Duration: Sep 2021 - Mar 2024

Project Scale: $120M

Executive Summary

The Core Challenge or Opportunity: Existing facility was nearing end-of-life and unable to meet stringent new water quality standards. Opportunity to implement cutting-edge treatment technologies.

My Strategic Action: Led the project from conceptual design through commissioning, overseeing a design-build contractor and ensuring minimal disruption to ongoing operations.

The Headline Result: Delivered a state-of-the-art facility that increased treatment capacity by 20%, reduced operational costs by 15%, and met all new compliance standards.

Strategic Impact & Business Acumen

Financial Oversight: Full P&L responsibility for the $120M project. Implemented rigorous change order management and value engineering processes.

Profitability & Margin Enhancement: Negotiated favorable terms with equipment suppliers and optimized construction sequencing, resulting in a 5% increase in project profitability.

Business Development & Client Relations: Exceeded client expectations, leading to the utility district awarding the firm a sole-source contract for their next capital improvement project.

Alignment with Firm's Goals: Showcased the firm's expertise in advanced water treatment technologies, enhancing its market position.

Leadership & Team Management

Team Structure & Your Leadership: Directed a project management office of 15, and provided oversight for a design-build joint venture of 100+ personnel.

Mentorship & Talent Development: Empowered a lead process engineer to evaluate and pilot an innovative membrane filtration system, which was successfully adopted.

High-Stakes Communication & Stakeholder Alignment: Presented regular progress updates to the Utility District's Board and navigated concerns from local community groups regarding construction impacts.

Conflict & Crisis Resolution: When a key piece of custom-fabricated equipment was delayed, I led a rapid re-sequencing effort with the contractor to mitigate schedule impact, limiting the delay to only 2 weeks.

Technical Complexity & Innovation

The Defining Technical Challenge: Retrofitting new advanced treatment processes (e.g., ozone and UV disinfection) into the existing plant footprint while maintaining continuous operation of the old plant.

Your Innovative Solution: Utilized 3D modeling and BIM for clash detection and meticulous construction planning, enabling complex tie-ins and minimizing operational downtime.

Key Metrics & Quantifiable Outcomes

  • Budget: $120M Budget vs. $118.5M Final Cost (1.25% under budget)
  • Schedule: Achieved Substantial Completion on Mar 1, 2024, as per original contract date, despite equipment delays.
  • Safety: 1.2M man-hours with an LTIR of 0.15 (significantly below industry average).
  • Quality/Client Satisfaction: Exceeded all performance guarantees for water quality; client provided exceptional CPARS rating.
  • Team Performance: Successfully integrated owner, design-builder, and operations teams into a cohesive unit.
  • Financial Result: Achieved a 10% profit margin, exceeding the 8% target, and secured a $20M follow-on project.
